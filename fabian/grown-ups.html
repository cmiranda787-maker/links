For grown-ups

This site is made for kids to enjoy and for grown-ups to trust. Here is how it keeps Fabian, and every visitor, safe.

What this site collects

Almost nothing. This site has no accounts, no contact form for kids, and no newsletter pop-ups. The one thing that collects anything is the Art Mail Club signup form, and it asks for a grown-up's email only. We never ask for a child's name, age or address.

The signup form is a Google Form embedded on the Art Mail Club page. Signups land on the same list the club has always used.

No tracking, no ads

There is no advertising on this site and no analytics that follow individual visitors. Videos from YouTube are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode, so they don't set tracking cookies until played.

Comments live on Instagram

Comments are off everywhere on this site. If you want to say hi, the conversation lives on Instagram, where it is moderated.

First name only

Only Fabian's first name appears anywhere on this site — in the words, the picture captions, and the page details. No last name, no town, no school, no birthday.

Contact email coming soon — the family will add it.
