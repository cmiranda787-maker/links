The 7 lessons we found

From David's Secret Diary: A Bible Adventure for Kids by Angel M. Pabón.

  1. God sees your heart, not your size.
  2. Courage starts before the battle.
  3. Real strength comes from God.
  4. Friends matter, choose them wisely.
  5. Even kings make mistakes.
  6. Saying I'm sorry matters.
  7. God can restore your story.
